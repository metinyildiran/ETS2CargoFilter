ETS2 CARGO FILTER - r4 / 0.1.0-preview.4
Supported: ETS2 1.61.1.9, Windows 64-bit (exact executable check).

ENGLISH
1. Close ETS2 and back up your profiles/save files.
2. Steam > ETS2 > Manage > Browse local files.
3. Copy ETS2CargoFilter.dll into bin/win_x64/plugins inside that game folder.
   Create the plugins folder if it does not exist. Keep other plugins intact.
4. Choose ONE interface installation:
   - Workshop: subscribe to ETS2 Cargo Filter and enable it in Mod Manager.
     Do not also enable the manual .scs copy.
   - Manual: copy ETS2CargoFilter.scs into Documents/Euro Truck Simulator 2/mod
     (use the redirected Documents folder if yours is on OneDrive).
     Enable ETS2 Cargo Filter in Mod Manager.
5. Enter your career and open Cargo Market. An owned truck and trailer are required.
6. The filter changes the list; confirming an offer assigns the matching trailer.

Workshop subscriptions do NOT install DLL plugins. The DLL is required with
either installation method. The plugin follows the language selected in ETS2.
Convoy testing is pending. Game updates may require a new plugin release.
To remove: close the game, disable/unsubscribe the UI, remove only
ETS2CargoFilter.dll from plugins and the manual .scs if you installed it.

TURKCE
1. ETS2'yi kapatin ve profil/kayitlarinizi yedekleyin.
2. Steam > ETS2 > Yonet > Yerel dosyalara goz at yoluyla oyun klasorunu acin.
3. ETS2CargoFilter.dll dosyasini bin/win_x64/plugins klasorune kopyalayin.
   plugins klasoru yoksa olusturun. Diger eklentilere dokunmayin.
4. Arayuz icin TEK yontem secin:
   - Atolye aboneligini kullanip modu Mod Yoneticisi'nde etkinlestirin, veya
   - ETS2CargoFilter.scs dosyasini Belgeler/Euro Truck Simulator 2/mod altina
     kopyalayip etkinlestirin. Belgeler OneDrive'daysa oradaki klasoru kullanin.
   Atolye ve elle kurulmus .scs kopyasini ayni anda etkinlestirmeyin.
5. Kariyere girip Kargo Piyasasi'ni acin. Kendi cekiciniz ve dorseniz gerekir.
6. Filtre yalniz listeyi degistirir; teklif onayinda ilgili dorse atanir.

Atolye DLL kurmaz; her iki yontemde de DLL zorunludur.
Convoy testi henuz tamamlanmadi. Oyun guncellenince yeni eklenti gerekebilir.
